- Scope and mode: replace the visual world of `/` and `/install`; Persuade.
- Audience and job: non-technical self-hosted n8n/VPS owners must understand
  what Relmio does, trust its boundaries, and start the local installer.

  request-bound chat demo.
- Proof on hand: the real two-lane relay mechanism, existing safety controls,
  checked-in setup screenshots, and the working hosted chat integration. Do
